Lighthouse Shift Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026. Kyriakos Kokkinidis develops Lighthouse Shift. This pre-release policy describes the planned Android game. Service sections apply to builds that enable the corresponding features.

Progress on your device

Guest play stores settings, completed shifts and best scores on your device so you can continue offline. Clearing app storage removes this local copy.

Play Games

When connected to Google Play Games, the game uses your player identifier to associate achievements, scores and saved progress with your Play Games profile. Saved Games transfers progress to Google so you can restore it on another device. You can manage or delete game data in your Play Games settings. Clearing local storage alone does not erase a cloud save.

Ads and privacy choices

Google AdMob may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Consent choices and regional rules affect ad personalization. Where available, use the privacy options in the game to revisit your choice. Android settings also let you manage your advertising ID. Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Gameplay analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase helps the developer understand tutorial completion, shift results and retries. Events may include the level number, attempt count and result. The SDK may also process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location and usage events according to the build configuration and applicable consent choices. Firebase data disclosures.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment. The game uses the product ID, purchase token and purchase status to verify and restore the Lighthouse Keeper Pack. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the game.

Retention, security and requests

Local progress remains until cleared or the app is removed. Google services transmit data over encrypted connections and retain records according to the service, account settings and applicable requirements. Purchase records may be retained to restore purchases or meet legal obligations. See Google’s privacy policy and Google Account data controls.

For questions, access or deletion requests, email Kyriakos Kokkinidis at ky.kokkinidis.developer@gmail.com with the subject “Lighthouse Shift privacy”. Identify the feature concerned so the developer can locate records under their control or direct you to the relevant Google control. Please do not send passwords or payment details.
