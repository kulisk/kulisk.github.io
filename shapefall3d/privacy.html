Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Kyriakos Kokkinidis develops SHAPE / FALL (ShapeFall 3D), Android package com.kulisgames.shapefall3d. This policy explains local game data and the Google advertising services included in the app.

Local progress and preferences

Scores, stage and daily results, tutorial progress, and settings are stored on your device. The game does not upload this progress to a developer server. No game account, cloud save, purchases or online leaderboard is required. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress; we do not hold a server copy.

Advertising and third-party data

Earlier versions without advertising do not include the advertising-related processing described below.

Advertising-enabled Android versions use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for occasional interstitial ads between runs and optional rewarded ads. Completing a rewarded ad can grant one continue in the current run. Core gameplay remains available without an internet connection; ads and online privacy messages require a connection.

Google's SDK may collect and share your IP address (including an estimate of general location), app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic information such as app/SDK performance, and device/account identifiers including the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's SDK data disclosure. We do not run a separate gameplay analytics service.

Your choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required. When available, open Settings → Privacy & Support → Ad Privacy Options to revisit them. Declining personalization does not necessarily eliminate all advertising or all SDK processing. Rewarded ads are optional; Retry and Main Menu remain available.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's privacy settings. Removing local game data does not itself delete information already processed by Google; use Google's privacy controls and support channels for provider-held data. You can contact the developer with privacy questions or requests.

Permissions and security

The game uses vibration for optional feedback and internet/network access for advertising and consent. Advertising SDK dependencies also declare identifiers and normal background/service permissions. The app does not request contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location access. Local progress security depends on your device and operating system.

Children

The game is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or its advertising services, contact us so we can investigate.

Retention and updates

Local progress remains until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Google handles retention and deletion of information it processes under its own privacy policy. We may update this policy when the app or its services change, publishing the revised policy at this location.
