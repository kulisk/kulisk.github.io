Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Kyriakos Kokkinidis ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game ShapeFall 3D. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ShapeFall 3D.

1. Information We Collect

ShapeFall 3D does not currently collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share personally identifiable information.

The game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, address, precise location, contacts, photos or files, or payment information.

2. Local Game Data

ShapeFall 3D stores certain gameplay information locally on your device in order to provide core game functionality. This may include high score, best streak, completed targets, games played, game settings, tutorial completion status, sound preferences, and vibration preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Internet Connection

The current version of ShapeFall 3D does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay. The game does not currently use online accounts, cloud saves, multiplayer services, online leaderboards, or online analytics.

4. Advertising

The current version of ShapeFall 3D does not contain third-party advertising.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of the game, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information may be processed by advertising providers and how users can manage their privacy choices.

5. Analytics and Tracking

ShapeFall 3D does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising tracking, or behavioral profiling technologies. We do not currently use the game to track users across other applications or websites.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of ShapeFall 3D does not intentionally integrate third-party services that collect personal information through the game. Future versions may introduce services such as analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud saving, or Google Play services. If such services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Device Permissions

ShapeFall 3D does not request access to sensitive device information or permissions that are unnecessary for gameplay. The game may use standard device functionality such as vibration when enabled by the user. No personal information is collected through vibration or similar device functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

ShapeFall 3D is not specifically designed or directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the current version of the game does not collect personal information from users, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through another means, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information collected through ShapeFall 3D because the current version of the application does not collect personal information.

10. Data Security

Because ShapeFall 3D stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on the user's device, the security of this local information also depends on the security of the user's device and operating system. We take reasonable steps when developing the application to avoid unnecessary collection or exposure of user information.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored by ShapeFall 3D is kept locally on your device until you clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or the game itself resets or replaces that information. Because this information is not transmitted to our servers, we do not maintain an external copy that requires a separate deletion request.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ShapeFall 3D changes or when new features, services, analytics tools, advertising systems, or legal requirements are introduced. Any updated version will be published on this page and the Effective Date above will be revised.
